Practicus AI documentation has moved

The public documentation site has been retired. Current documentation is available inside your Practicus AI environment, where it matches the version you are using.

Open the documentation

  1. Open your Practicus AI home page.
  2. Open the upper-right menu.
  3. Select Help.

The Help menu opens the private, version-matched documentation for your environment.

Open a version directly

The private documentation URL is generated from the Practicus AI version:

https://practicus.company.com/docs/v<version-with-dashes>/

For example, version 26.4.2 becomes:

https://practicus.company.com/docs/v26-4-2/

Replace practicus.company.com with your Practicus AI host. The version segment is the platform version with periods replaced by dashes and a v prefix.

If you cannot access your Practicus AI environment, contact your platform administrator or Practicus AI support.
